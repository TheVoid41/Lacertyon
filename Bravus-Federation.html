Information Regarding the Community

The Bravus Federation is the second strongest and second most well known nation in all of Lacertyon. It is also the second oldest nation in Lacertyon since just like the Imperium, it predates Lacertyon itself. The Bravus Community was founded in 2025. The Federation operated on many platforms in the past yet now it is on Lacertyon.

The nation operates not only on Lacertyon but it also operates on platforms like Polytoria. The main goal of the Bravus Federation is to save The World of Lacertyon from tyrants and apocalyptic events.

We are the main rival of the Imperium

Main Lore of the Bravus Federation

The Bravus Federation is a major superpower located in Ersumia. It has territories on almost all of the continents and is a rival of the Imperium. It is the second oldest nation on Ersumia with the first one being the Imperium. It is a democratic and capitalist nation of the east. The government is made up of ministries and the leader is a President.

Today the Federation is fighting the grand war of preservance. A global conflict that if not stopped in a year will cause the planet to be destroyed. The current year in lore is 1986. The lore universe is called Lacertyon.

Ministries of the Federation

The Government of the Bravus Federation is divided into Ministries. Those Ministries serve various different purposes such as law enforcement or creating propaganda. The Ministries act as civil branches of the Federation which means that all of them are joinable.

List of current ministries: Ministry of Law Enforcement, Ministry of Propaganda.

Military of the Federation

The Federation is one of the most well armed nations in all of Lacertyon. The Military of the Federation is the second most well organised and successful combat force in the whole world of Lacertyon. It is divided into 3 smaller specialised forces that act like combat branches which means all of them are joinable. To join a branch you have to enlist in the discord server of the Federation available on the top of this page.

Those Branches are: Federal Ground Force, Federal Air Force and the Federal Naval Force.

The Federal Ground Force

The Federal Ground Force sometimes referred to as FGF is the biggest force in the Military of the Bravus Federation Its purpose is to fight on the ground and push the front line along with performing special operations behind enemy lines.

The Federal Ground Force consists of: The National Army, Mechanised Troops and Special Task Force.

The Federal Air Force

The Federal Air Force sometimes referred to as FAF is the smallest force in the Military of the Bravus Federation Its purpose is to fight in the air and help the ground force push the front line along with assisting the naval force in their operations.

The Federal Air Force consists of: The Bravus Air Fleet, The Bravus Naval Air Support and The Air Special Operations
