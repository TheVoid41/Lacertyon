Information Regarding the Community

The Hydrux Empyreum is one of the strongest and most well known nation in all of Lacertyon. It is also the newest centre nation in Lacertyon since it was founded in September, 2026. The Empyreum operates on Lacertyon since it was made after the founding of The World of Lacertyon.

The Hydrux Empyreum operates not only on Lacertyon since it also has a coordination guild on Polytoria and host events on ROBLOX and Minecraft. The Empyreum operates on so many platforms to not only have more variety but also to recruit more members.

Main Lore of the Hydrux Empyreum

The Hydrux Empyreum is located on the continent of Usertiava and has the biggest amount of territory on that continent. It has the strongest and biggest navy in Lacertyon. The Empyreum is made up of provinces called Hydruxiens. In total there are 10 Hydruxiens in the Empyreum. Hydrux is known for only attacking when provoked and not causing conflict without a justification. It trades with every side and doesn't support anyone in the Grand War of Preservation.

We aim to become the richest and most influential nation in all of Lacertyon. We also want to control two continents that we are on. We also want to overtake the Imperium in Members.

Halls of the Empyreum

The Government of the Hydrux Empyreum is divided into Halls. Those Halls serve various different purposes such as law enforcement or province management. The Halls act as civil branches of the Empyreum which means that all of them are joinable.

List of current halls: Hall of Justice.

Military of the Empyreum

The Empyreum is one of the most well armed nations in all of Lacertyon behind Bravus. The Military of the Empyreum is also one of the most well organised and successful combat force in the whole world of Lacertyon. It is divided into 3 smaller specialised forces that act like combat branches which means all of them are joinable. To join a branch you have to enlist in the discord server of the Empyreum available on the top of this page.

Those Branches are: Hydrux Ground Force, Hydrux Air Force and the Hydrux Naval Force.

Hydrux Ground Force

The Hydrux Ground Force sometimes referred to as HGF is the second biggest force in the Military of the Hydrux Empyreum. Its purpose is to fight on the ground and push the front line along with performing special operations behind enemy lines. It is mainly an attack force so it usually stays in the combat areas.

The Hydrux Ground Force consists of: The Hydrux Main Army, Mechanised Troops, The Hydrux National Army and The Hydrux Special Operations.

Hydrux Air Force

The Hydrux Air Force sometimes referred to as HAF is the smallest force in the Military of the Hydrux Empyreum. Its purpose is to fight in the air and assist the ground force push the front line along with assisting the navy in their operations. It is mainly a support force so it usually stays near other forces.

The Hydrux Air Force consists of: The Hydrux Main Air Fleet, The Hydrux Air Support Fleet and the Hydrux Special Air Operations.
