The things all members of the imperium should know.

Information Regarding the Community

Imperium Imperterritus is the Strongest and most well known nation in all of Lacertyon. It is also the oldest nation in Lacertyon since it predates Lacertyon itself. The Imperial Community was founded in either 2024 or 2025 depending on what do you consider the true Imperium. The Imperium operated on many platforms in the past yet now it is on Lacertyon.

The nation operates not only on Lacertyon but it also operates on platforms like Polytoria. The main goal of the Imperium is to have full control over the world and create a utopia using innovative technology.

Fun Fact: Imperium Imperterritus was called Imperium Proelium for a short time due to the founder not knowing much latin.

Main Lore of the Imperium

Imperium Imperterritus or the Fearless Empire is the most feared and brutal superpower that occupies around 2/3 of the Ersumian continent. The Imperium came about after a group of powerful warlords decided to unite and form an organised regime in the year 587. At first the Imperium was a small republic and the warlords were leading the nation through many victories but the warlords wanted to turn the republic into a kingdom and make themselves the sole leader. This led to many assassination attempts made by warlords to make themselves the only survivor and thus the only leader. The last warlord declared himself the king and made the republic turn into a kingdom.

Today the Imperium is fighting the grand war of preservance. A global conflict that if not stopped in a year will cause the planet to be destroyed. The current year in lore is 1986. The lore universe is called Lacertyon.

Imperial Creations

The Engineering Column is the Column responsible for creating, updating and maintaining games of the Imperium. All developers in it are volunteers and don't get paid. The Development Roadmap can be found on the Imperial Discord Server. The games of the Imperium can vary from simple raid and training games onto full on nuclear plants.

Imperial Columns

The Imperial government is divided into Columns. They serve many different functions and have many different ways to join them.

List of all Imperial Columns:

  • External Column
  • Engineering Column
  • Knowledge Column
  • Management Column

Management Column

The Management Column consists of the:

  • Imperial Administration and Management Sub-Column which consists of province administrators, the parliament, the senate and the nation administration council;
  • Imperial Internal Affairs Sub-Column which consists of Border Operations Corps, Inspector Corps, Internal Enforcement Corps and the Internal Investigation Corps;
  • Imperial Civil Sub-Column which consists of the Logistical Corps, City Services Corps, Economical Corps and the Industrial Corps;
  • Imperial Colonial Management Sub-Column which consists of the Colonial Enforcement Corps, Colonial Administration Council and the Colonial Resource Management.

Engineering Column

The Engineering Column consists of:

  • Imperial Construction Sub-Column which consists of the Combat Construction Corps and the Civil Construction Corps;
  • Imperial Visual Sub-Column which handles textures in games and propaganda in lore;
  • Imperial Design Sub-Column which handles concept art in games and architechture in lore;
  • Imperial Sculpturing Sub-Column which handles models in game and the rim in buildings;
  • Imperial Software Engineering Sub-Column which handles scripts in games and software in lore.

Knowledge Column

The Knowledge Column consists of the:

  • Imperial Research Sub-Column which consists of the Nuclear Research Corps, Quantum Research Corps and the Anatomical Research Corps;
  • Imperial Space Sub-Column which consists of the Rover Corps, Rocket Corps, Space Ship Corps and the Space Station Corps;
  • Innovation Sub-Column which consists of the Warfare Innovatory Corps, Chemical Innovatory Corps and the Biological Innovation Corps.

External Column

The External Column consists of the:

  • External Affairs Sub-Column which consists of the External Investigation Bureau and the Diplomatic Commitee;
  • External Diplomatic Affairs Sub-Column which consists of the Diplomatic Corps and the Embassy Management Corps.

Imperial Armed Forces

The Imperial Armed Forces (IAF) is the attack and defensive force of the Imperium. It is divided into the Ground Force, Air Force and Naval Force. It is the most active part of the Imperium and the one that is the easiest to join.

To join, visit the Imperial Armed Forces Discord Server.

Ground Force

The Imperial Ground Force consists of the:

  • Imperial Exercitus
  • Mechanised Corps
  • Ranger Corps
  • Heavy Infantry Corps
  • Colonial Defence Corps
  • Scorching Environment Corps
  • Frozen Environment Corps
  • Mountainous Environment Corps
  • Ground Nurse Corps
