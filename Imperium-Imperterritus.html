Information Regarding the Imperium

Imperium Imperterritus is the Strongest and most well known nation in all of Lacertyon. It is also the oldest nation in Lacertyon since it predates Lacertyon itself. The Imperium was founded in either 2024 or 2025 depending on what do you consider the true Imperium. The Imperium operated on many platforms in the past yet now it is on Lacertyon.

The nation operates not only on Lacertyon but it also operates on platforms like Polytoria. The main Goal of the Imperium is to have full control over the world and create a utopia using innovative technology.

Fun Fact: Imperium Imperterritus was called Imperium Proelium for a short time due to the founder not knowing much latin.

Main Lore of the Imperium

Imperium Imperterritus or the Fearless Empire is the most feared and brutal superpower that occupies around 2/3 of the Ersumian continent. The Imperium came about after a group of powerful warlords decided to unite and form an organised regime in the year 587. At first the Imperium was a small republic and it the warlords were leading the nation through many victories but the warlords wanted to turn the republic into a kingdom and make themselves the sole leader. This led to many assasination attempts made by warlords to make themselves the only survivor and thus the only leader. The last warlord declared himself the king and made the republic turn into a kingdom.

Today the Imperium is fighting the grand war of preservance. A global conflict that if not stopped in a year will cause the planet to be destroyed. The current year in lore is 1986. The lore universe is called Lacertyon.
