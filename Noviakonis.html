Information Regarding the Community

The Federal Republic of Noviakonis is one of the most peaceful nations in all of Lacertyon. It is also one of the oldest nations in Lacertyon since it was founded in 2024 before the establishment of Lacertyon. The Federal Republic of Noviakonis mainly operates on Lacertyon.

Main Lore of the Federal Republic of Noviakonis

The Full name of Noviakonis is the "Federal Republic of Noviakonis". Its short name is "Noviakonis" or "Novia". The abbreviated name is "NVKN", "NVK", "NV" and "NK". It is capitalist, industrialist and democratic. It uses the 24 hour time like the rest of The World of Lacertyon. It is an atheist nation but that may change just like any other aspect of this page since change is a constant in Lacertyon. The name of the currency is "Novipound" - "NP". The national symbols are: the allamanda flower, the novian rabbits and the national dish are pancakes. People there drive on the right side of the road but some rural roads may still use the left side. The average skin colour there is white and the average hair colours are: brown, blonde and black.

They aim to stay peaceful, gain control over the whole Imoderiav sub-continent through diplomacy and become richer than any other nation in Lacertyon.

Military of Noviakonis

The Noviakonis Military is one of the worse militaries on the Asecantyrian continent. The Military of the Federal Republic is also meant to be a defensive force and doesnt have many troops. It is divided into 3 smaller specialised forces, but only one of the forces is a combat branch so only one is joinable. To join that branch you have to join the discord server of that branch.

The three specialised forces are: The Noviakonis National Guard (the only real combat branch).

Noviakonis National Guard

The Noviakonis National Guard sometimes referred to as NNG is the biggest force in the Military of the Federal Republic of Noviakonis. It acts as the ground force of the Federal Republic. Its purpose is to fight on the ground and push the front line along with performing special operations behind enemy lines. It is mainly an attack force so it usually stays in the combat areas.

The Noviakonis National Guard consists of: The Federal Infantry Corps and the Federal Armored Corps.
