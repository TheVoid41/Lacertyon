Information Regarding the Community

The Validus Confederation is one of the most militarised and destabilised nations in all of Lacertyon. It is also one of the newest original nations in Lacertyon since it was founded in 2026 months before the establishment of Lacertyon.

The Validus Confederation mainly operates on Lacertyon.

Main Lore of the Validus Confederation

The Validus Confederation is located on the continent of Asecantyria, the sub-continent of Imoderiav and has a relatively below average amount of territory on that continent. It has the strongest and biggest military on the Imoderiav Sub-Continent. A citizen of validus is called a validian and their national colours are black and green. The Confederation is made up of dozens of small militias/para-military groups which also means that the power is decentralised. In total there are 24 militias and para-military groups in Validus. Validus is known for being extremely decentralised and destabilised and the nation usually only unites during wartime. One of the militias is led by an ambitious commander hoping to unite the confederation and that started the war with noviakonis in hopes of keeping the nation in a state of unity which will make his goal easier.

The Confederation aims to take control over the whole sub-continent. Many Commanders in Validus want to unite the whole Confederation. The Validus Confederation aims to secure all borders and become wealthier than most of the nations on the continent.

Military of the Validus Confederation

Since Validus is a decentralised military confederation, it does not have civil branches and everything is built around the military. The fact that it is decentralised means that all of the militias/para-military groups use their own military forces and just put them together during wartime. All milias/para-military groups need to have the same "template" for their militaries since if one of the things from the "template" is lacking then the group wouldnt survive. Some of the groups have more than just the things from the "template" but those are exceptions and will not be mentioned in the "template" below.

The "template" is:

  • Ground Force;
  • Military Police Force;
  • Command Force;
  • Air Force (wealthier groups);
  • Naval Force (those groups with river/lake/sea access).
