Information regarding Venumet

Overview

The People's Republic of Venumet is the best communist nation on Polytoria. It is a heavily industrialised nation with closed borders. It was founded in October of 2026 by IAMTHEVOID41 on Polytoria. The nation operates on many games but it also has its own such as a border game and a training game. The People's Republic of Venumet consists of two main branches: the civil one and the combat one. Lore

The goal of the People's Republic of Venumet is to bring polytoria to its former glory and unify its people. It is the greatest communist power on polytoria with lore, frequent events and games. The People's Republic of Venumet aims to become the strongest nation group on Polytoria. It despises the Royal Nation of Polytoria and the Polytorian Empire saying that the power is meant to be given to the people and not a monarch.

Additional Information regarding the People's Republic of Venumet

  • You can join the Communist Party only if you meet the requirements.
  • All nations in Polytoria are allowed to fight with us. If you want to be our ally you must have the same ideology as we do.
  • Lore accurate clothing can be found on the guild store.
  • The People's Army of Venumet is the main source of activity in the People's Republic of Venumet. Joining it will make this group more fun for you.
  • You can't be in both a civil branch and a combat branch at the same time. This is done to avoid confusion during promotions.
