Information Regarding Lacertyon

Lacertyon is a world-building project set in the year 1986.

The project takes place on a planet with many nations that people can join.

Project Components

  • Lore Drops
  • Central Lore Drops
  • National Broadcasts
  • National Lore Drops
  • Nation Information Booklets

And other smaller resources and details are also part of the project but are too minor to deserve their own spot on this list.

The Nations of Lacertyon

The nations of Lacertyon are divided into categories. These categories reflect each nation’s importance and activity level. Official names remain the original names used when a nation joined Lacertyon.

Centre Nations

The main nations of Lacertyon. They are the most important nations in Lacertyon.

  • Imperium Imperterritus
  • Bravus Federation
  • Hydrux Empyreum

Major Nations

The Major Nations in Lacertyon. Major Nations are important but less prominent than Centre Nations.

  • There are no major nations in Lacertyon at this time, check back later.

Regular Nations

The Regular Nations in Lacertyon. They are basic nations with discord servers and that are active.

  • Federal Republic of Noviakonis
  • Validus Confederation - Work in Progress

Honorary Nations

The Honorary Nations in Lacertyon. They are semi-part of the Universe since they want to be more independent.

  • People's Republic of Venumet

Background Nations

Background Nations have very little or no lore, no active Discord server, and minimal worldbuilding.

  • Aequalis Commonwealth
  • Invictus Kingdom
  • Stiriacus Republic
  • Pernimius Empire
  • Ultrix Tsardom
  • Actinosus Union
  • Insuperabilis Dominion
  • Kingdom of Ganaya
