Information Regarding Lacertyon

Lacertyon is a world-building project set in the year 1986.

The project takes place on a planet with many nations that people can join.

Project Components

  • Lore Drops
  • Central Lore Drops
  • National Broadcasts
  • National Lore Drops
  • Nation Information Booklets

And much more!

The Nations of Lacertyon

The Nations of Lacertyon are divided into categories. This list has the categories, the nation names and the links to their discord servers. A nation being in a specific category is defined by its activity, and importance to the world of Lacertyon.
