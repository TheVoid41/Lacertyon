Information Regarding Lacertyon

Lacertyon is a world-building project set in the year 1986.

The project takes place on a planet with many nations that people can join.

Project Components

  • Lore Drops
  • Central Lore Drops
  • National Broadcasts
  • National Lore Drops
  • Nation Information Booklets

And much more!

The Nations of Lacertyon

The Nations of Lacertyon are divided into categories. This list has the categories, the nation names and the links to their discord servers. A nation being in a specific category is defined by its activity, and importance to the world of Lacertyon. Since nations can change parts of their names pretty often, Lacertyon formally uses the original names which are the names of the nations when they joined Lacertyon.

Centre Nations

The main nations of Lacertyon. They are the most important in Lacertyon.

  • Imperium Imperterritus
  • Bravus Federation
  • Hydrux Empyreum

Major Nations

The Major Nations in Lacertyon. They are less important than the centre nations and have proven to be powerful unlike the centre nations which were blessed by the creator.

  • There are no major nations in Lacertyon at this time, check back later.

Regular Nations

The Regular Nations in Lacertyon. They are basic nations with discords servers and that are active.

  • Federal Republic of Noviakonis
  • Validus Confederation - Work in Progress

Background Nations

The Nations with barely and or no lore, no discord server and no actual body. They only have names, colours on the map and sometimes flags.

  • Aequalis Commonwealth
  • Invictus Kingdom
  • Stiriacus Republic
  • Pernimius Empire
  • Ultrix Tsardom
  • Actinosus Union
  • Insuperabilis Dominion
  • Kingdom of Ganaya
